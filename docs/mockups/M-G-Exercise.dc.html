Barbell bench press

Some experience · Barbell, bench, rack

ChestFront shouldersTricepsCoach-verified

The main upper-body push. Press the bar from your chest to straight arms while your back stays tight on the bench.

Taught by Ananya Iyer · Reviewed by Rohan Desai

Add to TodaySave exercise
Step by step
  1. Eyes under the bar, feet flat, shoulder blades pulled together.
  2. Grip a little wider than your shoulders.
  3. Lower the bar to the middle of your chest, elbows at about 45 degrees.
  4. Press up and slightly back until your arms are straight.
Common mistakes

Bouncing the bar off the chest. Elbows flared wide. Hips lifting off the bench.

Safety

Set the safety arms in the rack, or train with a spotter. Never use a thumbless grip.
