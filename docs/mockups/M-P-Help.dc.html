Getting started

How do I join my coach’s program?

Open the invite link your coach sent. Sign in or create an account, and the program appears in Today.

Can I use FitSphere without a coach?

Yes. Every lesson in the library is free to watch. Add drills to Today from any lesson.

Why do you need my date of birth?

FitSphere is for adults 18 and over. We check age once at sign-up.

Training and logging

Who sees my session logs?

You, and the coach of the program the session belongs to. Logs are marked self-reported.

How is adherence worked out?

Sessions completed divided by sessions scheduled up to today.

The AI guide

Where do AI answers come from?

From published, coach-verified lessons, which it links. Without a matching lesson, it explains the idea only and labels the answer.

Can the AI help with an injury?

No. It will not diagnose. It suggests you stop, see a doctor or physiotherapist, and tell your coach.

How lessons are reviewed

What does Coach-verified mean?

A coach wrote the lesson and a second coach checked technique and safety. Sample lessons say Sample until a coach replaces them.

Your data

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Delete account. We remove your profile and logs.
