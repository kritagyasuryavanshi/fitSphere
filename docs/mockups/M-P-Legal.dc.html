What we collect

Your name, email, date of birth, sport choices, goals, equipment and session logs. Injury notes only if you add them.

AI processing

The AI guide runs on Anthropic, a processor outside India. It gets your sport, level and goals. It gets injury notes only if you say yes, separately. It never gets your name, email or phone.

What coaches see

A coach sees your logs and onboarding answers only while you are in one of their programs. When you leave, access ends at once.
