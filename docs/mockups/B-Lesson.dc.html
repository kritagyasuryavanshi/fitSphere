Step by step

  1. Set your stance. Feet shoulder-width, lead foot forward, hands at your cheeks, chin tucked.
  2. Extend straight from the guard. No wind-up. The punch starts where the hand already is.
  3. Turn the fist over. Palm faces down as the arm straightens; lead shoulder rises to cover your chin.
  4. Snap it back. Return on the same line, as fast as you threw it.

Common mistakes

Dropping the rear hand

Touch your rear glove to your cheek on every rep.

Pulling back to load

A wind-up shows the punch early. Throw from the guard.

Leaning forward

Keep your head behind your lead knee.

Flaring the elbow

Keep the elbow down and in so the punch travels straight.

Safety

Warm up your shoulders first. Do not snap the elbow to a hard lockout. Wrap your hands before any bag work.
