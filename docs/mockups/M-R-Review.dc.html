Catching the jab

Block the jab with the rear palm, then counter at once.

Steps
  1. Rear palm open, in front of the chin.
  2. Meet the jab early with a short push.
  3. Return the jab or a cross straight away.
Common mistakes

Reaching out to the punch. Slapping it down.

Safety

Partner throws light, slow jabs. Gloves on.
