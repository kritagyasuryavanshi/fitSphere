I'm a beginner. How do I stop dropping my right hand when I throw the jab?

AI explanation · based on 2 coach-verified lessons

This is the most common jab mistake, so you're in good company. The rear hand drops because your body is trying to balance the punch. Three fixes, in order:

  1. Touch and throw. Before each jab, touch your right glove to your cheek. Keep it there until the jab is back. [The Jab, step 4]
  2. Go slower. Throw at half speed for one round. Speed hides the drop; slow reps show it. [Stationary jab drill]
  3. Film one round. Watch it back at 0.5×. You'll see exactly when the hand leaves.

Step 3 is a general suggestion, not from a FitSphere lesson.
