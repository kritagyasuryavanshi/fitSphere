Kettlebell swing

Intermediate · 6 min · Kettlebell

Builds punching powerCoach-verified

A hip hinge that drives the bell with your glutes. The same hip snap powers a hard cross.

Taught by Ananya Iyer · Reviewed by Rohan Desai

A typical dose

3 × 15 · 16 kg

Rest 60 s between sets. Your coach sets your own dose.

Add to TodaySave exercise
Step by step
  1. Feet a little wider than hips, bell in front.
  2. Hike the bell back between the legs.
  3. Snap the hips forward. Arms only guide.
  4. Let the bell float to chest height.
Common mistakes

Squatting instead of hinging. Lifting with the arms. Rounding the back.

Safety

Learn the hinge first. Start light. Stop if your lower back hurts.
