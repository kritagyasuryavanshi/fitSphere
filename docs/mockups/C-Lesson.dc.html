The Jab

Beginner · 8 min · No equipment

Coach-verified Demo content

Your lead hand's straight punch. It finds the distance, sets up every combination and keeps the other boxer honest.

Taught by Vikram Rathore · Reviewed by Rohan Desai · 1,284 athletes learning

Step by step −
  1. Stance set: hands at cheeks, chin tucked, knees soft.
  2. Extend straight from the guard. No wind-up.
  3. Turn the fist over; lead shoulder covers the chin.
  4. Snap back on the same line.
Common mistakes +

Dropping the rear hand · pulling back to load · leaning forward.

Safety +

Warm up the shoulders. No hard elbow lockout. Wrap hands for bag work.

Why does my jab feel slow?

AI explanation

Most slow jabs start with a wind-up. Throw from the guard, relax the shoulder, and think about the snap back, not the reach.
