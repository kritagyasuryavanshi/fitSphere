Muscle Builder

8 weeks · 4 days a week · about 60 min · Full gym

An upper and lower split for people with some gym experience. Each week you add a rep or a little weight. Week 5 is lighter so your body can recover.

Ananya IyerStrength and fitness coach, Bengaluru. View profile

The weekly split

Week 1 shown · rest days between

Monday · chest, back, shoulders

Upper A

  • Barbell bench press4 × 8
  • Chest-supported row4 × 10
  • Overhead press3 × 10
  • Lat pulldown3 × 12

Tuesday · legs, glutes

Lower A

  • Back squat4 × 8
  • Romanian deadlift3 × 10
  • Leg press3 × 12
  • Calf raise3 × 15

Thursday · back, chest, arms

Upper B

  • Pull-up or assisted4 × 6–10
  • Incline dumbbell press3 × 10
  • Cable row3 × 12
  • Curl and triceps pushdown3 × 12

Friday · legs, core

Lower B

  • Deadlift3 × 5
  • Walking lunge3 × 10 each
  • Leg curl3 × 12
  • Plank3 × 45 s

How you progress

  • Each workout shows what you lifted last time and a small target to beat it.
  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add 2.5 kg.
  • Ananya sees your logs and adjusts when you stall.
