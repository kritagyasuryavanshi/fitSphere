## Problem Statement
People who want to learn a sport today stitch together YouTube for technique, a fitness app for workouts, WhatsApp for a coach, and memory for progress. Nothing connects "learn this skill" to "practice these drills" to "my coach saw my session". FitSphere v1 connects those three steps for Boxing, with Strength & Conditioning (S&C) as the integrated support category.

## Demand Evidence
None yet. The founder answered "No evidence yet" to the demand question. Every v1 decision below is a hypothesis until real coaches and athletes use the product. The success criteria and the assignment exist to produce that evidence.

## Status Quo
- Athletes: free YouTube videos with no structure, no safety review, and no link to a training plan.
- Coaches: programs sent as WhatsApp messages, PDFs or spreadsheets; adherence tracked by asking athletes.
- Market (thin evidence, web search 2026-11-04): India has venue/coaching players (KheloMore/Machaxi), school and academy coaching (TIDA Sports), new coach-management SaaS (Lynk.coach) and AI plan apps (Pocket Coach). No one clearly owns structured, coach-verified skill learning connected to training.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Primary athlete:** an adult (18+) in an Indian city who is a boxing beginner or early intermediate, trains at home or a gym, and wants structure instead of random videos.
- **Primary coach:** an independent boxing coach or S&C coach who already has athletes and content, and runs programs on WhatsApp.
- **Wedge:** a founding coach publishes real lessons and programs; their athletes follow them on FitSphere and log sessions; the coach sees adherence and gives feedback. The public library and AI assistant grow from that same verified content.

## Constraints
- India-first, mobile-first, low-data. Phones first, desktop second.
- Production quality: no placeholder pages, no dead buttons, no fake data. Sample content is labelled as sample.
- Adults only (18+) in v1. India's DPDP Act requires verifiable parental consent for minors; that work moves to the academy phase.
- YouTube embeds for video in v1. No paid video hosting yet.
- Founder accepts a longer timeline in exchange for three complete pillars.

## Change, 2026-11-05: three categories
The owner added general gym fitness: weight loss, muscle building (hypertrophy), strength and general fitness. There are now three categories, each with its own library: Boxing, Strength & Conditioning (S&C for boxers, unchanged), and Gym & Fitness (new). Gym logging uses sets × reps × load, shows last time's numbers, and suggests the next small increase. Body weight and height are optional, self-reported, and reach the AI only with separate consent. Meal plans and calorie targets stay out of v1 (nutrition advice); open question for the owner.

## Change, 2026-11-05: Coach Marketplace is in v1
The owner asked for a Coach Marketplace: discover, compare, shortlist and hire coaches, with packages, payment, verified reviews and a coach business dashboard. It is designed (canvas rows "Hire a coach" and "Coach business") and the owner moved it into v1 as milestone M4. AI moves to M5, launch to M6. Build risks: an empty marketplace before about 20 coaches, payments with split payouts and refunds in India, credential checks by an admin, and written consent for client stories. Rules in the design: client statistics come only from FitSphere records; only clients with a paid, finished engagement can review; coaches cannot pay to rank higher; payments stay on FitSphere.

## Premises
1. **Content and cold start are the main risks, not code.** Coaches solve both: they bring reviewed content, athletes, and willingness to pay. (Revised after the second opinion.)
2. **Two connected categories at launch:** Boxing (primary) and S&C (supporting, integrated, not a generic gym app). The data model stays multi-sport so cricket, football and MMA slot in later without a rebuild.
3. **Mobile-first web app (PWA)** for v1, not native apps.
4. **Adults only (18+)** in v1.
5. **Real-user evidence before Phase 2 work** (marketplace, payments, academies).

## Recommended Approach: A, expanded to three connected pillars
The founder expanded Approach A so v1 ships three complete, connected pillars.

### Pillar 1: Video Learning Library
- Public pages for Sport > Discipline > Skill > Drill/Exercise, filterable by difficulty.
- Each lesson: YouTube embed, explanation, step-by-step technique breakdown, common mistakes and corrections, safety guidance, related drills, prerequisites.
- Search and filters: sport, discipline, skill, difficulty, equipment, duration, environment, and drill type (technical, conditioning, mobility, recovery).
- Signed-in athletes can save lessons and mark them complete.
- Content workflow: draft → in review → published. Only published content is public or reachable by AI. Every item shows its author coach.
- **Who publishes:** only the admin grants the coach or reviewer role. Coaches write drafts and submit them. Only a reviewer or admin can publish, and nobody can approve an item they wrote. Database policies enforce these rules, not just the UI.
- **Sample content:** seed items carry an `is_sample` flag. They show a "Sample" label instead of "Coach-verified", and they stay out of the AI index. A coach replaces each sample through the normal review workflow.
- S&C items carry "supports" tags (for example "boxing footwork power"), so boxing pages surface relevant S&C work.

### Pillar 2: AI Instructional Assistant
- Answers how-to questions about skills and exercises, step by step, with mistakes and corrections, in beginner-friendly language.
- **Grounded retrieval:** answers draw on published, non-sample FitSphere content and cite the lessons and drills they use, with links.
- **When no library content matches:** the assistant may explain general concepts only, labelled "AI explanation, no library source". It never prescribes drills, sets, loads or progressions without a cited library item. It suggests asking a coach instead.
- Recommends library videos and drills, and builds learning paths only from items that exist in the library.
- Personalises by the athlete's sport, experience level and goals from onboarding.
- **Labels:** every AI answer is visibly marked "AI explanation". Library content shows "Coach-verified". AI never presents its own drill as verified and never invents credentials.
- **Safety:** no injury or medical diagnosis; refers to a qualified professional; respects stated limitations; refuses unsafe progressions.
- **Cost control:** per-user daily question limit; usage logged per user.
- **Personal data:** onboarding asks for separate, explicit consent before limitations or health notes are used for AI personalisation. Without consent, the AI gets sport, level and goals only. With consent, it gets only the fields the question needs, never name, email or contact details. The privacy policy names Anthropic as a processor outside India.

### Pillar 3: Coach-Led Training
- Coach profile: bio, sports, experience, coaching style, and published content. Experience is labelled "self-reported" until a verification process exists.
- Coach program builder: weeks → sessions → items (drill + prescription), mixing boxing and S&C items.
- Assignment by invite link, shareable on WhatsApp. An athlete can hold several active programs at once.
- **Today dashboard:** one view that merges today's boxing and S&C sessions from all active programs.
- Session screen: warm-up, items with video, prescription, rest timer, mark done, log results.
- **Sport-specific logging:** each drill declares its metrics. Boxing: rounds × duration, combinations, technique self-rating. S&C: sets × reps × load, distance × time, RPE. Logs are marked self-reported.
- Coach review: coach sees athletes' logs and adherence, and leaves feedback the athlete sees.
- **Coach data access:** a coach sees an athlete's logs and onboarding data only while that athlete is enrolled in one of the coach's programs. When the athlete leaves or the coach removes them, access ends at once. Past feedback stays visible to the athlete.
- Basic progress: sessions completed, adherence per program, personal records, all derived only from real logs.

### Technical shape (house defaults, confirmed at build start)
- Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) on Vercel; installable PWA.
- Supabase: Postgres with row-level security, Auth (email magic link + Google), pgvector for AI retrieval.
- AI: Claude via the Anthropic API, with the model chosen at build time. Server-side only; the API key never reaches the browser.
- Roles: athlete, coach, admin/reviewer.
- Tests: unit and database-policy tests in CI; end-to-end tests for each journey below; an AI evaluation set.

### Explicitly out of v1
Meal plans and calorie targets, academies, minors, phone OTP, Hindi, native apps, 3D, live classes, community, paid video hosting.

## Milestones (each with a Verify line)
1. **M1 Foundation.** App skeleton, Supabase schema for the multi-sport content graph and training, RLS, auth, roles, 18+ onboarding (sport, level, goals, equipment, availability, limitations), CI, Vercel preview deploys.
   **Verify:** `npm run typecheck && npm run lint && npm test && npm run build` pass. Policy tests prove:
   - an athlete cannot read another athlete's data;
   - the public cannot read draft content;
   - coach A cannot read coach B's athletes;
   - a coach loses access the moment an athlete leaves their program;
   - a coach cannot publish, and nobody can approve their own item;
   - only an admin can grant the coach or reviewer role.
   An end-to-end test signs up and completes onboarding. It is blocked if under 18, and the AI-personalisation consent is stored as a separate yes/no.
2. **M2 Video Learning Library.** Content pages, lesson pages with YouTube embeds, search and filters, save and complete, the authoring and review workflow, seed content clearly labelled as sample until coaches replace it.
   **Verify:** an end-to-end test browses Boxing > Technical Boxing > Jab > lesson, filters drills by equipment, saves a lesson and marks it complete, and both persist after reload. A draft lesson returns 404 publicly. A search for "jab" returns the jab skill and its drills. A sample item shows "Sample", never "Coach-verified".
3. **M3 Coach-Led Training.** Coach profile, program builder, invite link, Today dashboard, session screen, sport-specific logging, coach feedback, basic progress.
   **Verify:** an end-to-end test has a coach build a program with one boxing and one S&C session. An athlete joins by link. Today shows both sessions. The athlete logs rounds for boxing and sets × reps × load for S&C. The coach sees the logs and leaves feedback, and the athlete sees it. Progress numbers equal the logged data.
4. **M4 Coach Marketplace.** Coach discovery with filters, full coach profiles (credentials, packages, slots, portfolio), compare, shortlist, hire request, chat between client and coach, checkout (UPI, card, net banking), split payouts to coaches, monthly renewal and cancel, refunds, verified reviews, coach clients and earnings pages, admin credential checks.
   **Verify:** `npm test` and an end-to-end test prove:
   - an athlete hires a coach: request, accept, pay in test mode, and the coach's program appears in Today;
   - the payment webhook rejects a bad signature, and a repeated webhook does not create a second engagement (idempotency);
   - a refund marks the engagement refunded and the coach payout reverses;
   - payout = price minus the fee, exact to the paisa, for 3 sample prices;
   - only a client with a paid, finished engagement can post a review; a coach cannot edit or delete one;
   - chat is readable only by its client and coach; coach A cannot see coach B's clients or earnings;
   - profile statistics equal values computed from engagement and log tables, never coach input;
   - a credential shows "Credentials checked" only after an admin marks it.
   The payments go-live checklist (20 items) is run and Codex reviews the final diff before live mode.
5. **M5 AI Instructional Assistant.** Retrieval index built from published content only, cited answers, labels, learning paths, personalisation, safety rules, rate limit.
   **Verify:** `npm run eval:ai` runs a set of at least 30 questions (boxing, S&C, beginner concepts, injury, unsafe requests, off-topic, no-match) 3 times.
   - **Code-checked:**
     - every cited ID exists, is published and is not sample;
     - questions with matching content include at least one citation;
     - no-match answers carry the "no library source" label and no sets, reps or loads;
     - injury questions contain a referral and no diagnosis wording from a blocklist;
     - unsafe requests are refused;
     - an unpublished item is never retrieved.
   - **Judged:** an LLM judge with a written rubric grades clarity and correctness.
   - **Pass bar:** 100% on code-checked rules in all 3 runs and an average judge score of at least 90%.
   A unit test proves the daily limit blocks the next question.
6. **M6 Launch readiness.** Landing and about pages, privacy policy and terms, empty, loading and error states on every page, PWA install, performance on low-end Android.
   **Verify:** Lighthouse mobile performance ≥ 80 on the landing, library and Today pages. A route audit finds no placeholder page or dead button. A `/qa` pass shows no high-severity bugs.

## Open Questions
- **Who reviews content?** The founder cannot verify boxing technique. Proposal: the founding boxing coach is content lead, and a second qualified coach reviews.
- **Founding coach terms:** content ownership and licence, attribution, and what coaches get (featured profile now, revenue share later). This needs a written agreement before their videos go live.
- **YouTube dependency:** coaches' videos can be deleted or made private. v1 needs a check that flags broken embeds.
- **AI cost:** the daily question limit and how it maps to future Free/Plus/Pro tiers.
- **Legal:** the privacy policy and terms need review for DPDP compliance before public launch.
- Pricing stays a hypothesis until coaches and athletes are asked.

## Success Criteria
- Content before public launch: at least 20 boxing skills with lessons and at least 30 S&C drills, all published through review by founding coaches.
- Usage: at least 10 real athletes each log 3 or more sessions in their first week, without the founder chasing them.
- AI: at least 30% of active athletes ask the assistant a question, and at least half of those open a recommended library item.
- Willingness to pay: at least 2 coaches name a monthly price they would pay for the coach tools.

## Distribution Plan
Web service on Vercel, installable as a PWA. Each pull request gets a preview deploy. GitHub Actions runs typecheck, lint and tests. Production deploys from `main` only after the owner approves.

## Dependencies
- For M4: a registered business with GST registration (payment provider KYC), a coach agreement (fee, payouts, refunds, cancellations), the fee percentage, and the payment provider account (recommended: Razorpay with Route for split payouts).
- Founding coaches: one boxing coach and one S&C coach.
- Accounts: Supabase, Vercel, Anthropic API, and a Google OAuth client.
- Bun installed locally to restore the gstack tooling (not a product dependency).

## The Assignment
Before any code beyond M1, find one boxing coach and one S&C coach in your city. Sit with each for one hour this week. Ask how they send programs to athletes today, how they know who trained, and what they hate about it. Then ask them to be founding coaches, and ask each to list the first 20 skills or drills a beginner should learn. Bring their exact words back. They decide the seed content and test every premise above.

## Reviewer Concerns
One round of independent review ran on 2026-11-04. It used a fresh Claude subagent, because the gstack review helper needs Bun, which is not installed. It found 6 blocking and 4 minor issues. All 6 blocking issues were fixed in this doc: sample-content labelling, publish permissions, coach data access, AI no-match behaviour, a checkable AI pass bar, and consent for health data. The fixes were not re-reviewed.

Minor concerns, recorded and open for the plan stage:
1. **Embeddings provider:** the Anthropic API has no embeddings endpoint. Name one (for example Voyage) or use Postgres full-text search for v1. Re-index or delete when content changes.
2. **Metrics and records:** define a typed metric schema, a personal-record rule per metric type, and the adherence formula (completed ÷ scheduled sessions to date). Assert exact values in M3.
3. **Invite links:** make them per-program, revocable, and require sign-in plus 18+ onboarding before enrolment. The coach sees who joined.
4. **Missing milestone items:** add the broken-YouTube-embed check to M2 and DPDP legal sign-off to M5 as an owner gate. Decide whether v1 sends any notification, such as an email when a coach leaves feedback.
