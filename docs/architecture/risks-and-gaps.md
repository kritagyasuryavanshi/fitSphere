# FitSphere v1: Technical Risks and Missing Requirements

Written 2026-11-05. Each risk links to the board task that reduces it. Each open question has an owner answer slot.

## 1. Technical risks

Likelihood and impact: High, Medium, Low.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Board |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Scope.** v1 has six milestones including a payments marketplace. Months may pass before a real user trains. | High | High | Run a private beta with founding coaches after M3, while M4 is built. Decide in Q-01. | O-09 |
| R2 | **Access-rule bug leaks data.** A wrong RLS policy could show one athlete's health data to another coach. | Medium | High | One function (`coach_can_read`) for coach access; a pgTAP test per rule; Codex review per milestone. | M1-01, M3-04, M4-10 |
| R3 | **Payments.** Webhooks, retries, refunds, renewals and split payouts are easy to get wrong; money errors damage trust. | Medium | High | Amount set on the server; signature check; idempotency table; paise only; 20-item go-live checklist; test mode until approved. | M4-09…M4-16 |
| R4 | **Payment provider eligibility.** Split payouts (Route) need business KYC and approval, which can take weeks. | Medium | High | Start KYC now (O-04). Fallback: collect payments, pay coaches manually until Route is live. | O-04 |
| R5 | **Empty marketplace.** With few coaches, discovery and compare look dead. | High | Medium | Recruit coaches before M4 ships; hide filters with no results; show "new coaches joining" honestly. | O-06 |
| R6 | **Health data and DPDP.** Injury notes and body weight are sensitive; the AI provider is outside India. | Medium | High | Separate consent rows; minimum fields to the AI; legal review; data download and deletion. | M1-04, M1-08, M5-02, O-07 |
| R7 | **AI says something unsafe or uncited.** | Medium | High | Retrieval from published content only; code-checked rules; 30-question eval run 3 times; daily limit. | M5-01…M5-05 |
| R8 | **AI cost runs away.** | Low | Medium | Daily question limit per user; usage log with cost; alert at a set daily spend. | M5-03 |
| R9 | **YouTube videos vanish or go private.** | Medium | Medium | Daily check; author sees a flag; lesson shows a fallback. | M2-10 |
| R10 | **Date bugs.** "Today" depends on time zone; late-night joins can shift a whole program. | Medium | Medium | All date logic in `lib/dates.ts` (Asia/Kolkata), unit-tested at midnight and month edges. | M3-05 |
| R11 | **Slow on cheap Android phones.** Large photos and client JavaScript. | Medium | Medium | Server components, `next/image`, tap-to-load video, Lighthouse ≥ 80 gate. | M6-05 |
| R12 | **Phone designs not reviewed.** Phone screens are generated by a script. Reduced: the five most-used were reviewed and fixed (D2). | Medium | Low | Owner reviews key phone screens; each build task is checked at 390 px. | D2, S0-07 |
| R13 | **Off-platform payments and chat abuse.** Coaches may move clients to WhatsApp; chat may carry abuse. | Medium | Medium | Clear warning; report button; admin can read reported chats only. Needs Q-08. | M4-08 |
| R14 | **Single developer plus AI.** Knowledge lives in sessions. | Medium | Medium | Board, memory and plan in the repo; small commits; tests as documentation. | All |
| R15 | **Local tooling.** This PC runs Node 18; current tools need Node 22. | High | Low | Owner upgrades Node before S0-02. | O-03 |
| R16 | **Deleting accounts versus keeping payment records.** Tax rules require keeping invoices for years; "delete everything" conflicts. | High | Medium | Delete personal profile and logs; keep anonymised payment records for the legal period. Needs Q-10 and legal sign-off. | M1-08, O-07 |

## 2. Missing requirements (owner answers needed)

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Blocks | Default if no answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | Do we run a private beta after M3, before the marketplace ships? | Gets real users months earlier (R1) | Release plan | Yes, with founding coaches only |
| Q-02 | Is FitSphere free for athletes? Are there paid athlete tiers (the brief mentions Free, Plus, Pro)? | Changes data model and checkout | M4 | Free for athletes; FitSphere earns only the marketplace fee |
| Q-03 | Can a coach charge for an invite-link program, or are invite programs always free? | Two ways to join a coach: invite (free) and hire (paid) | M3-03, M4-07 | Invite programs are free; paid coaching goes through the marketplace |
| Q-04 | Fee %, payout timing, refund window, cancel rule, who pays GST, who issues invoices | Needed for payments and earnings | M4-09…M4-13 | None: must be decided |
| Q-05 | Booking is now designed (X-Book). Open: the cancel and move window, and whether in-person sessions need a deposit. | Refund rules | M4-17 | Full refund up to 24 hours before |
| Q-06 | Which events send email (feedback, request accepted, payment, renewal failed)? Any WhatsApp or push? | Notification scope | M3-14, M4-11 | Email only, for the five events listed |
| Q-07 | Does chat need to be instant, or is a refresh every 10 seconds enough? Photos or files in chat? | Realtime cost and moderation | M4-08 | Text only, refresh every 10 seconds |
| Q-08 | What happens when someone reports a review, message or coach? Who handles disputes? | Trust and safety for a marketplace | New task M4-18 | Report button; admin queue; owner handles disputes |
| Q-09 | Can coaches see each other's programs or content? Can a coach reuse library items by other coaches? | Builder library scope | M3-02 | Yes, any published library item; programs stay private |
| Q-10 | Data retention: how long are logs, chats and AI questions kept after an account is deleted or goes quiet? | DPDP and storage | M1-08 | Deleted on account deletion; payments kept anonymised as the law requires |
| Q-11 | Languages: English only in v1? | Copy and fonts | None | English only (Hindi out of v1) |
| Q-12 | Which product analytics and error tracking? Success criteria need weekly-return numbers. | No way to measure success otherwise | New task S0-08 | A privacy-friendly analytics tool and an error tracker, chosen at S0-08 |
| Q-13 | Domain name and sender email address | Sign-in emails and invite links | S0-06 | Must be decided before the first preview with real email |
| Q-14 | Which browsers and phones must work? | Test matrix | S0-07 | Last 2 versions of Chrome (Android, desktop), Safari (iOS), Edge |
| Q-15 | Admin tools beyond roles and credentials: hide a lesson, suspend a user, refund by hand? | Operating the marketplace | New task M4-18 | Suspend user and hide content only |
