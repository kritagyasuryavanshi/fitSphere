The owner said "use the defaults for M6" on 2026-11-22 ([[session-15]]). The plan is `docs/plans/m6-plan.html`.

- **Three steps.**
  - Step 1a: an invite beta for invited people aged 18 or more.
  - Step 1b: an open beta. It opens after a lawyer signs off the privacy notice and terms.
  - Step 2: the marketplace, after Razorpay KYC, GST and the rest of M4.
- **18+ beta.** Onboarding refuses anyone under 18. This pauses [[decision-no-age-limit]] until a lawyer designs parental consent.
- **Spend.** A domain costs about ₹800 a year. Resend and Sentry stay on their free tiers. Without a domain and an email sender, real users cannot sign in.
- **Ask AI** stays hidden in step 1. Later it gets a capped Claude key and the Claude-judged eval.
- **Hiring and chat** stay closed until reports and payments work. The database refuses them, not only the UI.
- **Grievance officer:** the owner, with a role address on the domain.
- **Outside advice:** one lawyer review before 1b, and a CA for GST before step 2. Both wait for the owner, because they cost money.

**Why:** real users' health data needs a signed privacy notice, and abuse needs reports before strangers can message each other.
**How to apply:** build the step 1 tasks on the board in order, starting with M6-10. Tasks that need spend or accounts wait on owner item O-11.
