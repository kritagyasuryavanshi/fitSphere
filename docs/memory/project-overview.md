fitSphere is a sports learning, training and coaching platform for adult boxing beginners and independent coaches in Indian cities.

v1 has three pillars ([[decision-three-pillars]]): a video learning library, a grounded AI assistant ([[ai-assistant]]), and coach-led training. The coach marketplace is in v1 too ([[marketplace]]).
