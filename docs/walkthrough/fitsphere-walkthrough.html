One site. Two doors.

Athletes and trainers use the same website and the same login. What each person sees depends on their role. This page walks through both journeys, then the path from this design to a working product.

Open the design canvas

Who uses it

Four roles. Everyone starts as an athlete. Only an admin can give the other roles.

Most people

Athlete

How they get in
Join free with an email link or Google. Adults 18+ only.
What they see
Today, Learn, Progress, Ask AI. Account settings behind their initials.

Trainer (coach)

How they get in
Apply on the For coaches page. An admin turns on coach tools.
What they see
A Coach header: Athletes, Programs, My lessons. A link back to the athlete site.

Reviewer

How they get in
An experienced coach that an admin also makes a reviewer.
What they see
Coach tools plus a Review queue for other coaches’ lessons.

Admin

How they get in
You, the founder.
What they see
People and roles, coach applications, the review queue.

Kabir trains boxing. Neha trains in the gym. Both follow the same path.

Where they meet

Athlete and trainer connect at three points: the invite link, the session log, and feedback.

sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Trainer
    participant F as FitSphere
    participant A as Athlete
    C->>F: Builds a program
    C->>A: Sends the invite link on WhatsApp
    A->>F: Signs up (18+) and joins
    F-->>A: Sessions appear in Today
    A->>F: Trains and logs sets, rounds, effort
    F-->>C: Dashboard shows the log and any flags
    C->>F: Writes feedback
    F-->>A: Note appears in Today and Coach feedback
    A->>F: Leaves the program (any time)
    F-->>C: Access to that athlete's data ends at once

The access rules

  • One login, roles decide the viewA trainer who also trains can switch with the “Athlete site” link.
  • Coaches see only their own athletesOnly while that athlete is in one of their programs. Leaving ends access at once.
  • Two coaches per lessonOne writes, another publishes. Nobody approves their own work.
  • Only an admin gives rolesCoach and reviewer roles never come from self sign-up.
  • Health data needs a separate yesInjury notes and body weight reach the AI only if the athlete turns that on.
  • Adults only in v1Under-18 sign-ups stop at a block screen and nothing is saved.

Phone and computer

Phone

The main way people will use it. Athletes get bottom tabs: Today, Learn, Progress, Ask AI. Coaches get Athletes, Programs, Lessons and Review. Visitors get a menu screen.

Installable as a home-screen app. No app store in v1.

Computer

The same site with a top navigation bar. Coaches will likely build programs and write lessons here, because the builder and editor are easier on a big screen.

From design to a working product

The design is done and clickable. Nothing is built yet. Each milestone below ships something you can test, and each has its own pass checks in the design doc.

  1. DesignDone55 desktop and 56 phone screens, all linked. Boxing, S&C and Gym & Fitness.
  2. PlanNextEngineering plan for M1 to M5: database tables, access rules, tests. Needs the stack decision first.
  3. M1FoundationSign-up with email link and Google, the 18+ check, the four roles, all access rules, and tests that prove them.
  4. M2LibraryBoxing, S&C and gym lessons with YouTube video, search and filters, save and complete, and the write, review, publish flow.
  5. M3TrainingProgram builder, invite links, Today, session and workout logging, coach dashboard, feedback, progress.
  6. M4AI guideAnswers from published lessons with links, safety rules, a daily question limit, and a 30-question test set it must pass.
  7. M5LaunchLanding, privacy and terms after legal review, phone install, speed on cheap Android phones, a full bug sweep.
