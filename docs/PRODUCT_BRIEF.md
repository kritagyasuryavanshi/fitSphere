# FITSPHERE — COMPLETE PRODUCT VISION & PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

> Source: the owner's product brief, pasted on 2026-11-04. Kept verbatim. Planning decisions that narrow it live in `PROJECT.md` and `MEMORY.md`.

## 1. Project Overview

I want to build **FitSphere**, an all-in-one sports learning, training, coaching, and performance platform.

FitSphere is not just a gym workout app or a fitness tracker. It is a universal platform where people can learn sports, develop athletic skills, follow structured training programs, find qualified coaches, track their progress, and improve their performance.

The platform should bring athletes, beginners, professional coaches, independent trainers, and sports academies together in one ecosystem.

**Core tagline:**

"One platform. Every sport. Every skill. Every goal."

The goal is to make learning and training in any sport easier, more structured, personalized, measurable, and accessible.

Build this as a serious, scalable commercial product that can eventually serve users across India and internationally.

---

## 2. Product Vision

Today, people use different platforms for different parts of their sporting journey:

- YouTube to learn techniques.
- Fitness applications to follow workout routines.
- Personal trainers for guidance.
- Sports academies for structured coaching.
- Separate applications to track their progress.
- Social media to discover coaches and training programs.

FitSphere should bring these experiences together.

A user should be able to open FitSphere, choose a sport, select a skill they want to improve, learn how to perform it, follow appropriate drills, generate a personalized training plan, find a coach if needed, and measure improvement over time.

The experience should be useful for both beginners and experienced athletes.

# 5. Core Learning Structure

One of the most important parts of FitSphere is its learning structure.

Every sport should be organized in a clear hierarchy:

**Sport → Discipline → Skill → Drill → Training Session → Training Program → Assessment**

Example:

Boxing
- Discipline: Technical Boxing
  - Skill: Jab
    - Drill: Stationary Jab
    - Drill: Moving Jab
    - Drill: Jab with Footwork
    - Drill: Jab Accuracy Practice
  - Skill: Cross
  - Skill: Hook
  - Skill: Defensive Movement

Cricket
- Discipline: Batting
  - Skill: Cover Drive
    - Shadow Batting
    - Front-Foot Movement
    - Target Practice
    - Throwdown Practice
    - Net Practice

Football
- Discipline: Ball Control
  - Skill: Dribbling
    - Cone Dribbling
    - Close-Control Dribbling
    - Directional Changes
    - Game-Situation Dribbling

Strength and Conditioning
- Discipline: Strength
  - Skill: Squat
    - Bodyweight Squat
    - Goblet Squat
    - Barbell Squat
    - Squat Progression

Each sport, discipline, skill, and drill should have its own dedicated learning page.

The structure should be easy to navigate and understandable even for someone who has never used a sports training platform.

# 9. AI Sports Training Assistant

AI should be one of FitSphere's major differentiators.

The purpose of AI is not simply to provide a chatbot. It should act as a personalized sports learning and training assistant.

### AI Training Plan Generator

Users should be able to describe their goals and receive a personalized training plan.

For example:

"I want to improve my boxing skills, build endurance, and train five days per week."

The assistant should consider:
- Selected sport.
- Experience level.
- Goals.
- Available training days.
- Available session duration.
- Equipment.
- Training environment.
- Existing training workload.
- Previous performance.
- User-provided limitations.

It should then create a structured plan using the platform's available drills, exercises, and verified learning content.

### Training Plan Details

A generated plan should include:
- Program name.
- Goal.
- Duration.
- Weekly schedule.
- Training days.
- Rest days.
- Session objectives.
- Exercises and drills.
- Sets and repetitions where appropriate.
- Duration.
- Intensity guidance.
- Progression.
- Recovery recommendations.

The plan should be editable.

Users should be able to regenerate or modify a plan when their goals, schedule, or available equipment changes.

### AI Sports Assistant

Users should be able to ask questions such as:
- How do I improve my jab?
- What drills improve batting footwork?
- How can I improve my sprint speed?
- What should I practice as a beginner football player?
- Explain the difference between strength and power training.
- What should I focus on in my next training session?

Answers should be understandable, structured, and relevant to the user's sport and experience.

### AI Safety and Reliability

AI must use FitSphere's approved learning material, drills, and exercises as its foundation.

It should not invent drills and present them as verified content.

It should:
- Clearly distinguish established guidance from suggestions.
- Respect user limitations.
- Avoid diagnosing injuries or medical conditions.
- Avoid unsafe training recommendations.
- Recommend qualified professional guidance when appropriate.
- Allow users to review and edit generated plans.
- Make it clear when information is uncertain.

Higher-risk training plans should require appropriate professional review.
